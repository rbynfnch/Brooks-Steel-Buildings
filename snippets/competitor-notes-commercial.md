# Competitor messaging notes: commercial and retail (owner-supplied analysis, unverified by Claude)
Competitors reviewed in the owner's analysis: RHINO, General Steel, Nucor Building Systems, Sunward Steel.
Claude could not open the competitor sites (network blocked), so these notes rely on the pasted analysis.

## Shared themes (what buyers are told)
Cost and value, speed of construction, design flexibility, open interior space, durability and low maintenance, future expansion.

## What it confirms in our draft
Our six benefit cards already cover speed, open space, adaptability, durability, customization. Plain wording, outcome-first, no stats.

## What it adds (proposed for the Commercial draft)
1. Appearance: a "Built to look like your business" section (storefronts, windows, doors, canopies, finishes, trim). [CONFIRM which options BSB can really supply]
2. Outcome language for open space: aisles, displays, inventory, equipment, reconfiguring later.
3. Trust strip under the hero: 30+ years, engineered for local snow and wind, 8 to 10 week turnaround, serving WY, UT, ID.
4. 3D designer: keep the standard band; add one sentence on what it does. [CONFIRM what it can configure vs what needs engineering]
5. Real projects with size, colors, use, town: needs real photos (later strip).
6. Example sizes: only if the owner supplies real, typical sizes BSB quotes.

## What we will not copy
- Published online prices (BSB quotes custom).
- "Cheaper/faster than conventional" percentages or energy-performance claims without a verified source.
- Anything implying a manufacturer relationship.
